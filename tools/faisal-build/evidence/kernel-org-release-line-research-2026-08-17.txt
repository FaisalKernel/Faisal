Relevant policy summary: mainline kernels are released approximately every 9-10 weeks; after a mainline release, it becomes a stable release line; longterm kernels are maintained release lines. Production qualification must therefore select and forward-port to a verified stable or longterm line rather than treating an rc development snapshot as production-ready.
